## About Me

I am an incoming **Western University Computer Science + Ivey AEO** student building at the edge of software, AI, finance, and company-building.

I started early because I wanted to understand how ideas become products, how products become systems, and how systems become companies. That path led me from founding **Top Score Tutoring** to leading technology at **Stellar Learning**, where I serve as **Deputy CTO** and helped scale the platform to **40,000+ active users**.

My work sits between engineering and strategy. I build full-stack products, experiment with AI workflows, and think deeply about how software can make complex systems easier to use. Recently, that has included work on AI-powered financial modeling tools, data visualization, student platforms, and product infrastructure across web and mobile.

I am especially interested in **AI, fintech, financial modeling, investing, venture-scale software, and the operating discipline behind great startups**. Western CS will sharpen the technical side. Ivey will sharpen the business side. I am focused on using both to build serious products with real users.
